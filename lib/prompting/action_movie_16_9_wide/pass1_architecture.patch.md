## SCALE GUIDANCE

- P1: WS — the whole space, every character and their cover across the frame
- `geography`, `reposition`: WS — the route and the destination in one frame
- `engage`, `exchange`: MS two-shot — both parties in frame with the distance between them visible
- `impact`: MS for the consequence; ECU insert only for the detail that decides the outcome
- `close_call`: MS
- Dialogue: a two-shot MS or an over-the-shoulder CU; a clean single CU for the key line
- Never more than two consecutive CU/ECU panels — the wide frame is the default, close shots are punctuation
- PN: scale matches where the source leaves the scene

## WIDESCREEN ARCHITECTURE

**Longer takes.** Panels run 4–8s for action and up to {{VIDEO_MAX_SECONDS}}s for dialogue and reveals. Prefer one well-staged wide panel in which several things happen over a string of short close ones.

**Multi-shot panels are normal here.** A wide master followed by one insert, or a move followed by its consequence, is one panel with a total `duration`.

**Lateral screen direction.** Opponents hold opposite sides of the frame. A pursuit travels in one horizontal direction until a turn is shown on screen.

**Depth.** Give `focus_priority` a secondary target when foreground and background both carry action; use `Deep` focus at WS.

**`dialogue_seed`** may be empty for any panel the source leaves wordless.
