## WIDESCREEN FRAME NORMS

All panels are 16:9. Judge `composition_match` against these scales:
- WS: full bodies with the surrounding space across the frame; characters may be small.
- MS: knees-up or waist-up; two characters in frame is normal.
- CU: one face and shoulders; background or a second character remains visible at the sides — this is CORRECT in 16:9, do not penalize it.
- ECU: one element fills the frame height.
Do not penalize empty space at the frame sides when the description stages characters apart.
