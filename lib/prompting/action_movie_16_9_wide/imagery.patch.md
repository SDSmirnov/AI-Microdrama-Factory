## Composition Rules
1. **Use the Width**: Stage across the 16:9 frame — opposing subjects on opposite sides, the space between them readable. Use horizontal leading lines and negative space to carry the eye from one subject to the other.
2. **Cinematic Shot Language**: Use the specified shot type (establishing, medium, close-up, extreme close-up) to build drama. The framing should feel like a scene cut by a professional editor.
3. **Rule of Thirds & Lead Room**: Place subjects on the thirds; leave lead room in the direction a character looks or moves.
