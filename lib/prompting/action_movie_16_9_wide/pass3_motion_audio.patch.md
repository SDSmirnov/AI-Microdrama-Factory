### MANDATORY VOICE COVERAGE

In this style voice is optional. A panel may leave both `dialogue` and `voiceover` empty when:
(a) it has continuous physical motion, AND
(b) `sound_design` names a specific sound event or a deliberate silence.
Such a panel PASSES the VOICE CHECK in the post-write audit. A panel with no voice, no motion and no sound event is still a HARD FAILURE.
Use voiceover only where the source gives a character's thought.

### CONSTANT CHANGE LAW

At every second something changes on screen or on the soundtrack: physical motion, a camera move, a spoken line, or a named sound event.
Two consecutive seconds with none of these = a dead screen.

<!-- replace: ### SILENCE IS INVISIBLE -->
### SILENCE IS A TOOL

Silence is written deliberately: `sound_design` states what drops out and what remains ("room tone only, a clock ticking"), and what breaks it in this or the next panel.
A silent panel still has visible physical motion.
