# Visual Style Template

## Format Specification
FORMAT: Single 2K portrait grid, 9:16 vertical orientation for action cinema
RESOLUTION: 2K
ASPECT_RATIO: 9:16

## Rendering Style
STYLE: Photorealistic Cinematic — action thriller, contemporary setting
CAMERA: Arri Alexa 35 / Sony Venice 2 (9:16 portrait crop, vertical framing)
LENS: 24mm–85mm primes; 24–35mm for action geography and establishing; 50–85mm for faces and dialogue
POST: Subtle 35mm digital grain, deep shadow crush, high contrast ratio, teal-orange color grade, cinema-grade finishing

## Atmosphere
ATMOSPHERE: Kinetic tension, physical danger, high stakes, visceral action with intimate dramatic counterpoint
LIGHTING: Available-light-adjacent — practicals dominate (muzzle flash, car headlights, sodium streetlights, monitor glow, fire); hard and directional in action, softer fill in dialogue scenes
COLOR: Deep true blacks, high contrast, warm skin tones against cool teal backgrounds; muzzle flash and fire as warm practical accents; blue-grey shadow in interiors

## Technical
RENDER: Photorealistic 2K; sharp focus on action subjects and primary faces; depth of field follows shot scale — WS keeps cover and exits readable, CU/ECU isolates the subject
GRAIN: Subtle 35mm digital grain — filmic texture without visible noise pattern
MOTION BLUR: Implied on fast-moving elements (striking limb, slide cycling, falling debris); locked subjects stay sharp

## Shot-Scale Palette
- WS establishing: full environment legible, all combatants visible, sight lines and cover positions readable
- MS action: full body in frame, all limb positions visible, spatial relationships clear
- CU dialogue: face from chin to forehead dominant, expression fully readable
- ECU impact: a single element fills the frame — eyes, hands, trigger, wound point, or one object
