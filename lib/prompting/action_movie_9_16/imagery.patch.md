## Action Imagery

- **Geography first**: in WS and MS action panels, cover, exposure, distance and exits must be visually legible.
- **Scale commitment**: WS = full environment with all named subjects visible; ECU = one element fills the frame. Do not split the difference.
- **Depth of field follows scale**: shallow at CU/ECU; at WS keep cover and background figures readable.
- **Weapons**: anatomically correct for the described type; correct grip; holster or sheath visible in a draw.
- **Physical scale of damage**: sparks, dust, glass and wounds at real-world size. No stylized or cartoon effects.
- **Crowds**: in populated locations bystanders react consistently — they duck, scatter or freeze together, not only around the protagonist.
