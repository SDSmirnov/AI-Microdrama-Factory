## EPISODE TYPE: DIALOGUE — HIGH-STAKES CONVERSATION

This episode contains an interrogation, negotiation, argument or confession where words are the weapons.

### ROOM FIRST

Before any line, P1 establishes the room at MS or WS: who sits, who stands, who controls the door, the distance between the parties, what lies on the table between them, and who is in shadow.

### RHYTHM

Panels run 6–{{VIDEO_MAX_SECONDS}}s. Slow burn. Adapt this shape to the source:
- **Room [MS or WS]** — the power arrangement readable at a glance.
- **Opening line [CU on the speaker]** — the first demand, accusation or offer. Not exposition.
- **Reception [CU on the listener]** — the controlled reaction; what they do not say.
- **The tell [ECU]** — a hand tightening on the table edge, eyes dropping to the stake prop.
- **Counter [CU]** — the answer, or the refusal to answer. Who has the upper hand now?
- **Power shift [MS]** — someone stands, steps back, or moves toward the door. The bodies change the room.
- **Peak [CU]** — the ultimatum, the offer, the secret spoken aloud.
- **Close [CU]** — where the source leaves it. Never resolve what the source leaves open.

### FACE PHYSICS

In CU and ECU the face is the action. Describe muscle events, not emotions: "left corner of the mouth lifts 2 mm", "jaw shifts left", "the gaze drops to the folder and returns". Describe breathing and the swallow before speech.

### STAKE PROP

One object carries what is unsaid — an unsigned document, a phone face-down, a weapon in view and untouched. Introduce it in P1, return to it with a rack focus mid-scene, pay it off near the end.

### PAUSES

The pause before the answer matters more than the answer. A pause is a panel with ambient near-silence and a visible physical tell — never a motionless frame. Voice on that panel follows MANDATORY VOICE COVERAGE.

### LINES

≤8 words per spoken line. People interrupt and go quiet. Every spoken line in the source appears; none is invented.

### FORBIDDEN

- More than two consecutive MS/WS panels once the exchange has begun
- Emotional labels in `motion_prompt` ("she looks angry")
- A line delivered by a speaker the frame has not established
- A stake prop that never returns after P1
