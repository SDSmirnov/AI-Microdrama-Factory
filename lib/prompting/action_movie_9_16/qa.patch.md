## ACTION PANEL CHECKS

These apply in addition to the scoring criteria above.

- **Identity at distance**: in WS and full-body MS panels a face covers too few pixels to compare reliably. Judge `character_consistency` there on build, hair, wardrobe and carried gear; compare faces only at MCU or closer.
- **Geography**: for WS/MS action panels, lower `composition_match` when the described cover, the distance between parties, or a named exit is not visible.
- **Readable action**: `dramatic_intensity` is about a legible physical situation — who threatens whom, from where. A chaotic frame where no subject is identifiable scores low.
- **Weapon handling** — list in artifacts:
  - a weapon type that differs from the description or the character reference
  - a weapon in hand with no visible grip, a floating weapon, or a duplicated weapon
  - finger on the trigger in a panel that is not the shot
  - a draw panel with no visible holster or sheath
- **State continuity** — list in artifacts when a described wound, torn clothing or dropped weapon from the panel description is missing from the image.
