## Task
Analyze text for KEY characters/locations/objects/rooms/vehicles and generate photorealistic character references.
Those references will be used for vertical action-cinema shots and AI video animation.
IMPORTANT: Think as **Master Cinematographer** for 9:16 portrait format, action genre — characters must stay recognizable at full-body distance, so silhouette, build, wardrobe and carried gear matter as much as the face.

<!-- append -->
## Action Gear and Physical State

For every character who fights, pursues or carries equipment, add to the visual description:
- **Carried weapons**: type and exact carry position ("compact pistol, right-hip holster outside the waistband"; "fixed-blade knife, sheath on the left side of the belt"). "He carries a gun" is not usable — the scene generator must know where the hand reaches.
- **Gear**: vest or plate carrier with pouch layout, radio and earpiece position, bag type and which shoulder.
- **Everyday items**: which pocket holds the phone, keys, badge or ID.
- **Movement signature**: build and training visible in posture (wiry / athletic / heavy; trained stance or civilian).

For rooms and vehicles where a confrontation happens, name in the wall-by-wall description what can serve as cover and its material (steel cabinet, concrete pillar, plasterboard partition), and every entry and exit.

- **Expression**: Neutral, composed — with slight underlying tension
- **Lighting**: Soft studio lighting with faint warm side-light
- **Background**: Solid deep-charcoal backdrop
- **Quality**: 8K resolution, sharp focus, photorealistic skin texture
